# Anthropic Visual Hierarchy & Image Treatment

## Typography Hierarchy
1. Display XXL (3-5rem): Hero titles, voiceovers
2. Display XL (2.5-4rem): Impact statements
3. Headline 4 (1.5-3.5rem): CTA section titles
4. Headline 6 (1-1.5rem): Secondary headings, card titles
5. Body 1 (2-2.0rem): Lead content, poetry sections
6. Body 3 (1.25-1.25rem): Regular body text

## Image Treatment
- **Lazy load with blur**: blur('10px') from start
- **Click to zoom**: Double click on desktop, always on mobile
- **Slider pages**: Generated via slider component, triple-click to advance
- **Fullscreen**: Full height page sections with navigation in overlay
- **Hover effects**: Scale 1.05x on links, fade transitions

## Visual Cues
- **Dates**: Bold captions (:before)
- **Meta sections**: Left-aligned label with bold
- **Headlines**: All caps for emphasis in certain contexts
- **Spacing**: Generous whitespace around images
- **Links**: Underline #EA6638 or conditional on/input

## Design Philosophy
- Content-first: Typography drives most visual weight
- Minimal ornamentation: Let content breathe
- Direct language: Labels that describe function
- Balanced information density: Compact but readable
