# Anthropic Color Palette
## Design System Colors

### Backgrounds
- **page--bg**: Nero (#141413)
- **ivory-medium**: #FAF9F5
- **ivory-light**: #F8F8F8
- **light-ivory**: #FAF9F8

### Text Colors
- **text-main**: Black (#000000) on light backgrounds
- **text-inverted**: White (#FFFFFF) on dark backgrounds
- **text-faded**: #6B7280 (gray scale on ivory)
- **text-body**: #374151

### Accents
- **swatch--cloud-light**: #E8EEF2
- **swatch--slate-light**: #64748B
- **swatch--slate-dark**: #1E293B
- **swatch--olive**: #A4D58A

### Interactive States
- **selected-text**: rgba(204, 120, 92, 0.85)
- **button-focus**: #D6B26C
- **hover-color**: Increases opacity by 15-20%

### Borders & Dividers
- **border-main**: #E5E7EB
- **border-subtle**: #F3F4F6
