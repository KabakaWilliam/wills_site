I train and build reliable AI agents.

I'm a third-year PhD student at the Oxford Internet Institute, advised by Joss Wright and Chris Russell. I study what models know about their own capabilities. I use that knowledge to make agents more reliable on long-horizon tasks.

I'm also a Research Scientist Intern at Thomson Reuters, working on post-training and harness optimisation for agents.
